The story

Built in the style of a Russian horse barn

✦

Castle and Country was designed in the style of a Russian horse barn, built for a Russian woman.

You can see that heritage in the great stone-and-timber barn, with its cupola and weathervane, its deep wraparound covered porch and its sliding barn doors, and in the storybook cottage of fieldstone and shingle that sits beside it.

Around them are open lawns, wooden post-and-rail fences and old hardwoods, all behind a gated gravel drive and a pair of stone pillars. More of the property's history will be shared here as the owners confirm it.
