The estate

A barn, a cottage and a lawn

A stone-and-timber barn designed in the style of a Russian horse barn, a storybook cottage beside it, and open grounds. Uses below are proposals; details to be confirmed are marked.

Reception · proposed

The barn

The estate's centrepiece is a stone-and-timber barn designed in the style of a Russian horse barn. A cupola and weathervane crown a steep, shingled roof, and a deep wraparound covered porch runs along its front.

Its big sliding barn doors open onto a timbered room hung with string lights. Proposed use: dinner and dancing on the porch and inside, with the lawn just beyond.

  • Stone walls with wood-clad dormers and diamond-pane windows
  • Sliding barn doors, live-edge wood siding and string lights
  • Covered porch on heavy timber posts for shade and shelter
  • Rain plan using the porch and barn: proposed, to be confirmed

Getting ready · proposed

The cottage

Beside the barn stands a storybook cottage of fieldstone and shingle, with steep rooflines, wood-clad dormers, diamond-lattice leaded windows, a tall arched window, a copper-roofed bay and an upper deck.

Proposed use: a bridal suite and getting-ready space on the morning of the wedding, with an optional overnight stay. Bedrooms and bathrooms: [Bedroom and bathroom count TBD].

  • Fieldstone walls and a steep shingle roof
  • Leaded diamond-pane windows and a copper-roofed bay
  • Upper deck overlooking the grounds
  • Overnight stay for the couple: proposed add-on, not yet confirmed
