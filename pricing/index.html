How it could work · Proposed

From list to date

  1. Join the list. Free, no payment, no obligation. You hear first when dates open.
  2. Tour the estate. Tours by appointment, once the county allows them.
  3. Hold a date. After county approval, a refundable $50 hold, credited to your deposit.
  4. Sign a contract. A 25% retainer at signing. Refundable in full if the venue is not open by the contract's outside date.
  5. Balance. Due 60 days before your date.

These terms are proposals and will be reviewed by the owners and their attorney before any money is taken.
