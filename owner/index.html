Demo dashboard. Every figure comes from adjustable assumptions or invented sample data. The season opens July 2027, so the first period modeled is July to December 2027, then the first full year, 2028. Venue fees are the owners' ($5,000 regular, $6,500 Saturday). Software (about $300 a month) and payment processing (about 3%) came from the brief. Everything else is a prototype assumption, and the property tax or mortgage line is a placeholder at 0.
