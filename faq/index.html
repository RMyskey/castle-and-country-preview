Questions

Frequently asked

Straight answers, including what we do not know yet.

When does Castle and Country open?

Our first season cannot begin before July 2027. Opening also depends on county approval, permits and inspections. July and August are hot, so we expect fall 2027 to be the first peak season.

What if it does not open on time?

Joining the list costs nothing. If a hold or deposit is ever taken, the proposed terms are that a hold is fully refundable until you sign a contract, and a contract deposit is refundable in full if the venue is not open by the contract's outside date.

How much does it cost?

$5,000 for a regular date (any day except Saturday) and $6,500 for a Saturday. A limited Founding Couples launch offer is proposed at 20% to 30% below those rates. See the pricing page. What the fee includes is still to be confirmed.

How many guests can the venue hold?

Capacity has not been confirmed. The owners will set ceremony, reception and total limits, and they will appear here once known. [Capacity TBD]

What is the story behind the property?

Castle and Country was designed in the style of a Russian horse barn, built for a Russian woman. More of its history will be shared as the owners confirm it.

What spaces are there?

A stone-and-timber barn with a covered porch, a storybook fieldstone cottage, open lawns, a well house and a gated gravel drive. Proposed uses are a ceremony on the lawn, a reception in the barn and on its porch, and getting ready in the cottage.

What if it rains?

The covered porch and the barn are the natural candidates for a rain plan. The exact plan is to be confirmed. [Plan TBD]

Can we stay overnight?

An overnight cottage stay is a proposed add-on. Sleeping arrangements and the price shown are placeholders. [Bedrooms TBD]

Can we bring our own caterer and bar service?

The owners have not yet set a vendor and alcohol policy. Any rules will follow county requirements and licensing. [Policy TBD]

Is the property accessible?

To be confirmed. The cottage entrance has steps, the drive is gravel and the grounds are open lawn. The owners will document accessible routes before launch. [Accessibility TBD]

Can we tour the estate?

You can request a tour now. Tours will be by appointment once the county allows them, and we will contact you. Please do not visit the property unannounced.

How does booking work?

Proposed: join the list, tour, place a hold once the county has approved opening, then sign a contract with a 25% retainer; the balance is due 60 days before your date. These terms are proposals, reviewed by the owners and their attorney before any money is taken.

Are pets welcome?

Policy to be decided. The property has friendly resident dogs who may say hello.
